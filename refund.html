Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2025

Last Updated: November 23, 2025

Introduction

At Jalwa Games, we strive to provide a fair and transparent gaming experience. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued and the process for requesting a refund. By using our Platform, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

Please read this policy carefully before making any deposits or participating in gameplay. If you have questions, contact our customer support team before proceeding.

1. General Refund Policy

A. Non-Refundable Transactions

Due to the nature of online gaming and prediction-based platforms, the following transactions are non-refundable:

  • Completed Bets: Once a bet is placed and the round has started or completed, no refund will be issued
  • Lost Bets: Funds lost during gameplay are not eligible for refunds
  • Bonuses and Promotional Credits: Bonus funds, referral rewards, and promotional credits cannot be refunded or withdrawn until wagering requirements are met
  • Voluntary Deposits: Deposits made intentionally and processed successfully are non-refundable unless an error occurred on our end
  • User Error: Refunds will not be provided for mistakes made by the user, such as selecting the wrong bet amount or game type

B. Refundable Transactions

Refunds may be issued in the following situations:

  • Duplicate Deposits: If the same deposit is processed multiple times due to a technical error
  • Failed Transactions: If funds were debited from your account but not credited to your Jalwa Games wallet within 24 hours
  • Unauthorized Transactions: If you can prove that a transaction was made without your authorization (subject to investigation)
  • Platform Error: If a technical glitch resulted in incorrect deduction of funds or game outcome discrepancies
  • Account Suspension (Minors): If an account is found to belong to a user under 18 years of age, deposits will be refunded to the source account

2. Eligibility for Refunds

To be eligible for a refund, the following conditions must be met:

  • The refund request must be submitted within 7 calendar days of the transaction date
  • You must provide proof of the transaction, including:
    • Payment receipt or transaction ID
    • Screenshots of bank statement or UPI transaction confirmation
    • Date and time of the transaction
    • Amount debited from your account
  • The transaction must fall under one of the "Refundable Transactions" categories outlined above
  • Your account must be in good standing (not suspended or flagged for fraudulent activity)
  • You must have contacted customer support and provided all requested documentation

Refund requests submitted after 7 days will not be considered unless exceptional circumstances apply (at the sole discretion of Jalwa Games).

3. How to Request a Refund

If you believe you are eligible for a refund, follow these steps:

Step 1: Contact Customer Support

  • Email us at support@jalwagames.com with the subject line "Refund Request"
  • Or use the in-app customer support chat (available 24/7)

Step 2: Provide Required Information

Include the following details in your refund request:

  • Your registered mobile number or email address
  • Transaction ID or payment reference number
  • Date and time of the transaction
  • Amount debited
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Screenshots or proof of transaction (bank statement, UPI confirmation, etc.)

Step 3: Wait for Review

  • Our support team will review your request within 5–10 business days
  • You may be asked to provide additional documentation or clarification
  • Once the review is complete, you will receive a notification via email or in-app message

Step 4: Refund Processing (If Approved)

  • If your refund is approved, the amount will be credited back to your original payment method within 7–14 business days
  • For UPI refunds, processing time is typically 3–5 business days
  • For bank transfers, processing time may take up to 10–14 business days depending on your bank

4. Non-Refundable Items and Activities

The following are explicitly non-refundable:

  • Game Outcomes: Results of any completed game, bet, or prediction round
  • Losses: Any funds lost during gameplay, regardless of the reason
  • Withdrawal Fees: Fees charged for processing withdrawals are non-refundable
  • Bonus Abuse: Accounts found exploiting bonuses or promotions will forfeit all funds without refund
  • Violation of Terms: Accounts suspended or terminated for violating our Terms & Conditions will not receive refunds
  • Internet Connectivity Issues: We are not responsible for losses caused by poor internet connection or device issues
  • User Negligence: Refunds will not be issued for failure to secure your account or protect your login credentials

5. Duplicate or Failed Transactions

A. Duplicate Deposits

If the same deposit is accidentally processed multiple times due to a technical error:

  • Contact customer support immediately with transaction proof
  • We will investigate and refund the duplicate amount within 7–10 business days
  • Only genuine duplicate transactions will be refunded (not intentional multiple deposits)

B. Failed Transactions

If funds were deducted from your bank account but not credited to your Jalwa Games wallet:

  • Wait for 24 hours, as some transactions may take time to reflect
  • If the amount is still not credited after 24 hours, contact support with transaction proof
  • We will trace the transaction with our payment gateway and credit your wallet or issue a refund within 7–14 business days

6. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe an unauthorized transaction was made on your account:

  • Contact customer support immediately
  • Change your password and enable two-factor authentication (if available)
  • Provide proof that you did not authorize the transaction
  • We will investigate the claim, which may take up to 14 business days
  • If our investigation confirms unauthorized access, a refund will be issued
  • If the investigation reveals that the transaction was made from your device or account, the refund request will be denied

Note: You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure. Jalwa Games is not liable for losses resulting from account sharing or failure to protect your password.

7. Platform Errors and Technical Glitches

In rare cases, technical errors or glitches may occur during gameplay or transactions:

  • If a game freezes or crashes mid-round, contact support with details (date, time, game type, bet amount)
  • If you believe the game outcome was incorrect due to a technical issue, report it immediately
  • Our technical team will review the logs and determine if an error occurred
  • If an error is confirmed, the affected bet amount will be refunded to your wallet
  • If no error is found, the original result stands and no refund will be issued

Important: Claims of technical errors must be reported within 24 hours of the occurrence. Delayed reports may not be eligible for review.

8. Refund Processing Time

Note: Refund timelines may vary based on your bank's processing time and payment gateway policies. Jalwa Games is not responsible for delays caused by third-party financial institutions.

9. Refund Method

Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method:

  • If you deposited via UPI, the refund will be sent to the same UPI ID
  • If you deposited via bank transfer, the refund will be sent to your registered bank account
  • If you deposited via credit/debit card, the refund will be credited back to the same card

In exceptional cases where the original payment method is unavailable, we may process the refund to your registered bank account after verification.

10. Cancellation of Withdrawals

If you have requested a withdrawal but wish to cancel it:

  • Withdrawal requests can be canceled before they are processed (usually within 24 hours)
  • Once the withdrawal is processed and sent to your bank, it cannot be reversed
  • To cancel, contact customer support immediately with your request

11. Disputes and Chargebacks

A. Dispute Resolution

If you disagree with a refund decision:

  • Contact customer support to escalate your case
  • Provide any additional evidence to support your claim
  • A senior support representative will review your case within 7 business days
  • The final decision made by Jalwa Games is binding

B. Chargebacks

Important Warning: Initiating a chargeback with your bank or card issuer without first contacting Jalwa Games customer support may result in:

  • Immediate suspension of your account
  • Forfeiture of any remaining balance in your wallet
  • Permanent ban from the Platform
  • Legal action to recover costs associated with the chargeback

We strongly encourage you to resolve any payment disputes through our customer support team before involving your bank or card issuer.

12. Contact Information for Refund Queries

For any refund-related questions or to submit a refund request, please contact us:

  • Email: support@jalwagames.com (Subject: Refund Request)
  • In-App Support: Available 24/7 through the customer support chat
  • Response Time: We aim to respond to all refund queries within 24–48 hours

Please provide all necessary documentation and information to expedite the refund process.

13. Changes to This Refund Policy

Jalwa Games reserves the right to modify or update this Refund Policy at any time. Changes will be communicated through:

  • Email notification to your registered email address
  • In-app notifications
  • Updates to this page with a revised "Last Updated" date

Your continued use of Jalwa Games after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Refund Policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

14. Final Notes

  • Jalwa Games is committed to fair and transparent refund practices
  • We encourage responsible gaming and urge users to only deposit amounts they can afford to lose
  • Refunds are processed at the sole discretion of Jalwa Games based on the circumstances of each case
  • All refund decisions are final and binding

If you have any concerns about our refund process, please do not hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. We are here to help!
